# Figure: Full-range single-gate error versus normalized angle

Infidelity of a single long-range Pauli rotation as a function of normalized angle x = θ/(2π) at L=12, separation 7, χmax=8. Lines show medians over three random initial MPS seeds and the RXX, RYY, and RZZ axes; light ribbons show the full min–max range over those nine combinations (not a statistical confidence interval). Values below 10⁻¹⁰ are shown at the plotting floor with unmodified database values.

Hybrid TDVP small-angle fits (10⁻⁴ ≤ x ≤ 10⁻²) give median exponent p = 1.97 with range 1.95 ≤ p ≤ 1.98 over nine seed–axis combinations (R² median 1.000). The θ² guide is shown only in the perturbative region and is not claimed beyond it.

At θ=π/2 (x=1/4): TDVP median infidelity 1.496e-01. At θ=π (x=1/2): TDVP median infidelity 1.927e-01. At θ=2π (x=1/1): TDVP median infidelity 1.690e-01.

Seeds: 11, 22, 33. MPO results refer to the tested zip-up implementation, not all possible MPO compression schemes.
