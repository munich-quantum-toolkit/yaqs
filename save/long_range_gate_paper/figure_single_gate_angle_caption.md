# Figure: Single-gate error versus angle

Infidelity of a single long-range Pauli rotation as a function of rotation angle at χmax = 8. Lines show medians over three random initial MPS and the RXX, RYY and RZZ rotations; whiskers show the full range. Hybrid TDVP exhibits approximately quadratic angle dependence, with a median fitted exponent p = 1.99 and a range 1.95 ≤ p ≤ 2.00 over the nine state–axis combinations. The variational-MPO results coincide with MPO zip-up at the plotted resolution.
